# Axis 8: Technical (Ingenieurwesen)

## 📋 OVERVIEW
The Technical Axis handles DIN standards, mechanical engineering documentation, and engineering blueprints.

## 🛠️ CORE COMPONENTS
- **DIN-Normung:** Integration of Deutche Institut für Normung standards.
- **Schaltpläne:** Standards for electrical and logical schemas.
- **Maschinenbau:** Terms related to mechanical construction.

## 🔑 KEY TERMS
- *Fertigungstechnik* (Manufacturing tech)
- *Konstruktionslehre* (Design theory)
- *Werkstoffkunde* (Materials science)
