# Axis 9: Medical (Medizintechnik)

## 📋 OVERVIEW
The Medical Axis governs the terminology for medical diagnostic equipment, patient safety protocols, and imaging technologies.

## 🔬 CORE COMPONENTS
- **Bildgebende Verfahren:** MRI, CT, and ultrasound terminology.
- **Diagnoseprotokolle:** Standardized medical assessment flows.
- **Patientensicherheit:** Failure mode analysis in clinical environments.

## 🔑 KEY TERMS
- *Röntgendiagnostik* (X-ray diagnostics)
- *Herz-Lungen-Maschine* (Heart-lung machine)
- *Klinische Validierung* (Clinical validation)
