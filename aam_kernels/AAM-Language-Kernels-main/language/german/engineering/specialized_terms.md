# Axis 10: Specialized (Fachtechnik)

## 📋 OVERVIEW
The Specialized Axis focuses on industrial automation, quality assurance processes, and highly specialized production technologies.

## ⚙️ CORE COMPONENTS
- **Automatisierung:** PLC (SPS), robotics, and control systems.
- **Qualitätssicherung:** Statistical process control and ISO 9001 integration.
- **Prozesstechnik:** Large-scale industrial process Management.

## 🔑 KEY TERMS
- *Speicherprogrammierbare Steuerung* (PLC/SPS)
- *Total Quality Management* (TQM)
- *Automatisierungsgrad* (Degree of automation)
